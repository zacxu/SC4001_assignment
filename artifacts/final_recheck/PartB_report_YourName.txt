Data, architecture interpretation and common protocol

Use the supplied 4,708/524/624 train/validation/test partitions and 64×64 grayscale images. Divide uint8 by 255
once, then normalize with training-pixel mean 0.5716994213 and population SD 0.1740975398. Numeric labels
are assumed 0=healthy, 1=pneumonia; the archive has no semantic metadata. Eight distinct images overlap
train/validation; three extra copies occur within test. No exact train/test or validation/test matches were found, but
patient independence cannot be checked without IDs.

Convolutions use SAME padding, no bias, then BN/ReLU; FC layers have bias. ReLU follows residual sums.
The final binary output is interpreted as an unrestricted logit, trained with BCEWithLogitsLoss; sigmoid≥0.5
predicts class 1. Adam, seed 42, 20-epoch cap, patience 4 and loss improvement 0.0001 apply throughout. Stop
on validation loss; save lowest validation classification error, then lower loss, then earlier epoch. Across tied
candidates, prefer the smaller rate/batch. No augmentation, weighting, resampling or refit; test is evaluated once
per selected model. Metrics use full-partition evaluation mode.

Why sensitivity and specificity are necessary

Validation is 74.24% positive: predicting pneumonia for everyone would yield 74.24% accuracy, 100% sensitivity
and 0% specificity. Sensitivity=TP/(TP+FN) measures detected positives; specificity=TN/(TN+FP) measures
correctly rejected negatives. LR 0.01 has higher sensitivity than 0.001 but far lower specificity (80.74% versus
97.04%), a trade-off hidden by one aggregate score.

Batch size also changes the update budget

For batches 16/32/64/128/256, updates per epoch are 295/148/74/37/19. Selected checkpoints receive
3,835/1,924/444/185/304 updates; batch 16 receives 12.6× as many as batch 256. Smaller batches alter
gradient/BN estimates, while larger batches make fewer updates per epoch. Gradient variance and BN effects
were not separately measured. This one-seed comparison selects the required training recipe, not a universal
batch-size ranking or an equal-update experiment.

B1 retains a 4×4 spatial FC head and dropout, but has the most parameters/FLOPs (67,713 head parameters).

not isolated.
