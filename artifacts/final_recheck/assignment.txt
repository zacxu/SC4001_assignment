Part A: Regression Problem

In this part, you will develop deep neural networks for predicting Singapore HDB resale prices. A complete
PyTorch baseline baseline.py that runs end-to-end, has been provided. We will use publicly available data
on HDB flat prices in Singapore, obtained from data.gov.sg. The original dataset is combined with other
datasets to include more informative features and they are given in the ‘hdb_price_prediction.csv’ file.
Open housing_baseline_data.zip and follow the README instructions to extract the source and data files.

Use the data gathered from 2017-2020 for training, in 2021 for validation and model selection, and in
2022 for final testing.

                                      near stations tend to fetch higher prices.

                                 Dhoby Ghaut is chosen as it is centrally located. Flats in the Central
                                         region are typically more costly.
 degree_centrality    Numeric       A metric (computed for the MRT station closest to the flat) that
                                        represents the degree of the node, i.e., how many edges are
                                     connected to the node. (Rationale: flats near ‘interchange’ stations -
                                            stations with more than 1 MRT line - offer more transport options
                                   and thus have higher value. Stations in the central areas tend to
                                    have > 1 MRT line too).
 eigenvector_central   Numeric       A more global metric than degree_centrality as it captures
  ity                                 neighbourhood information. When the eigenvector centrality of a

                                           too.
 flat_model_type      Categorical (Str)  Type of flat. See this reference for more details. You’re not expected
